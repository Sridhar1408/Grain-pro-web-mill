About Us

SRI MURUGAN FLOUR MILL has been serving the community since 1995 with traditional milling methods that preserve the nutrition and flavor of grains. Our family-owned mill takes pride in providing the freshest, highest quality flours and grains to our customers.

We source our grains directly from local farmers who share our commitment to quality and sustainability. Our homemade multigrain porridge mix has become a favorite among parents for its nutritional value and great taste.

Visit us to experience the difference that traditional milling makes in the taste and quality of your food.
